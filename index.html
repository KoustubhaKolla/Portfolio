Financial Performance & Stock Returns Analysis

Python, SQL | August 2026

I collected five years of revenue, net income, debt, equity, and stock return data for 100 public companies to test whether growth, profitability, and leverage are linked to stock performance.

I built SQL tables and used joins, filters, and grouping to combine datasets and segment companies by industry. In Python, I cleaned the data and calculated revenue growth, net profit margin, and debt-to-equity. Net profit margin had the strongest fit (R² 0.47), followed by revenue growth (0.38) and debt-to-equity (0.19).

View code on GitHub
61.4%average five-year return for companies with over 15% revenue growth, versus 27.8% for the rest

Mobile Phone Ownership, Internet Use & Digital Payments by Gender

Excel | August 2026

Using the World Bank Global Findex Database, I compared how men and women across countries own phones, use the internet, and make or receive digital payments.

I cleaned the data by gender and variable pair, then built scatterplots with quadratic trendlines and R² values. Phone ownership strongly tracked internet use, but its link to digital payments was weak. That suggests access alone does not drive adoption, so I flagged employment, bank account access, and literacy for further study.
